SHEET 01 / 03 SYSTEMS DESIGN · UFMFUS-5-2

Electric power screw press

Full-scale design of a die-forging press driven by a 15 kW AC induction motor through a single-stage helical gearbox. Selected a self-locking Tr50×8 trapezoidal power screw over a square thread for higher root strength and manufacturability, paired with a sacrificial phosphor-bronze drive nut.

  • Sized the gearbox (16MnCr5 case-hardened steel) using the AGMA method; verified bending stress against the fatigue limit.
  • Selected SKF tapered roller bearings for the combined radial and axial forging load.
  • Specified the PLC/VFD control logic and ran an FMEA to identify high-risk failure modes — buckling, thread stripping, over-travel.

SHEET 02 / 03 APPLIED THERMOFLUIDS · UFMFTS-30-2

CFD: flow separation in a 60° pipe bend

Ran 3D simulations in ANSYS Fluent for a 0.022 m pipe with a 60° bend, comparing laminar (Re = 1000) and turbulent (Re ≈ 17,300) regimes on a fully structured Hex8 mesh. Validated the turbulent velocity profile against Pitot-tube lab data using the k-ω SST turbulence model.

  • Ran a mesh-independence study, confirming convergence at 170,346 elements before extracting final data.
  • Increased the bend centreline radius from 50 mm to 150 mm and measured the resulting drop in minor loss.
  • Traced the mechanism: the wider radius kept the boundary layer attached at the inner wall, suppressing the Dean vortices that dissipate energy.

SHEET 03 / 03 IY421 · GEARBOX DESIGN

Gearbox design from first principles

Sized a spur gear pair to transmit 2,600 W and reduce speed from 1,500 RPM to 300 RPM. Worked through the full calculation chain by hand and in Excel — Lewis bending stress, dynamic factor, Hertzian contact stress — before selecting the shaft diameters and bearing life.

  • Compared three candidate materials (AISI 1045, 4140, 4037) against cost, machinability and environmental impact in a weighted decision matrix.
  • Selected AISI 1045 for its balance of strength and cost, hitting a factor of safety of 2.32–2.97 without heat treatment.
  • Validated the final design with a SolidWorks FEA static study of the assembled gear pair.
